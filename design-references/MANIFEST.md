# Design References — Seed Manifest (Curated from 2026 Trend Research)

**Purpose:** seed inputs for `artDirector.js` / `swipeFileEngine` design-DNA extraction (see `DESIGN_QUALITY_TASK.md`, Phase 3).
**Curation filter:** bold yet classy — high scroll-stop power, zero template smell, refined execution. Chosen so no two entries share palette logic, type mood, or layout energy.

**How to use:** source one image per entry (search query provided), save it in this folder using the suggested filename, then fill in the `Taste note` line with WHY you picked it (the taste note matters more than the image — a vision model can extract hexes/fonts on its own; only you can say what makes it good).

**Status:** ✅ = seed board generated (original SVG, in this folder). ⬜ = image not yet added.

**Note:** All 10 boards below are ORIGINAL generated seed boards (SVG, ~3 KB each) — not sourced imagery. They are safe to commit and to feed directly to a vision model for DNA extraction. Swap any of them for a curated external reference later if you want richer taste signal.

### Usage rules for Freebuff (persona set)
1. Persona styles are **opt-in per niche** — only assign when the niche genuinely maps to the tribe (see Best-for). A wrong-tribe product actively hurts conversion.
2. When a persona style fits, it may be used at FULL volume for that niche (no 10% cap — the whole niche audience is the tribe).
3. Persona boards are exempt from the "blend adjacent energies" rule only with trend-board entries (11 can blend with 9; 12 with 7/10) — never blend two personas.
4. Do not water these down: the edge IS the feature. If the critique loop scores them low on "classy," that is expected — score persona boards on tribal recognition and craft, not on mass-market polish.
