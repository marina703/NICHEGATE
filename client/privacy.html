NicheGate — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Operated by Nichegate (single-operator digital products business)

What NicheGate is

NicheGate is an internal operations console used by its owner to create, list and distribute digital products (e.g. PDF/XLSX/ZIP downloads) on marketplaces such as Etsy and Gumroad, and to publish content on social platforms such as Pinterest. NicheGate is not a consumer-facing service: visitors to this site do not have accounts and NicheGate does not offer any public sign-up.

Data we collect

  • Operational OAuth tokens. When connecting NicheGate to third-party platforms (e.g. Pinterest, Etsy), the owner authorizes the app and access/refresh tokens are stored server-side in an encrypted-at-rest database on the owner's infrastructure. These tokens belong only to the owner's own accounts.
  • Service data. Product data, listing metadata, sales metrics returned by the connected marketplaces, and internal automation logs, all belonging to the owner.
  • Server logs. Standard request logs maintained by the hosting provider (Railway) for security and availability.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or store personal data from end users visiting this site.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use tracking, advertising or analytics cookies on these public pages.
  • We do not access other people's Pinterest boards, pins or profile data beyond what the platform grants during the owner's own authorization.

Use of third-party platform data

Where OAuth scopes allow, NicheGate reads and writes data on the owner's own connected accounts (e.g. creating pins and boards for the owner's own content). No other user's data is requested. Third-party platform data is used solely to operate this business as registered with the respective platform and per its developer terms.

Data retention & deletion

Operational data is retained while the connected integrations remain active. Tokens and related operational data are deleted when an integration is disconnected, on platform-initiated credential revocation, or upon written request by the account owner or platform at marina@letsbloww.com.

Security

The operations console is protected by an authenticated session; public pages expose no data. Access tokens are stored server-side and never exposed in client code, logs or public assets.
