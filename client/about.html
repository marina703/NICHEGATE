NicheGate

A small, owner-operated digital products studio.

NicheGate creates, lists and distributes original digital deliverables — planners, checklists, trackers and templates (PDF / XLSX / ZIP) — for niche small-business audiences. Products are sold on Gumroad and on Etsy.

The NicheGate site itself is an internal operations console: there is no public storefront or sign-up here. Customer purchases, accounts and support happen on the marketplaces where the products are listed.

Our developer integrations (e.g. Pinterest, Etsy) connect only to the owner's own business accounts to publish the owner's own content and read that account's own performance data. See our Privacy Policy.
